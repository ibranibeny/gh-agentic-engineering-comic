Nothing extra: each model call is billed at that model's normal rate. Right. No Fusion fee, but every model call still counts at its normal rate.

What you learned

  • With Copilot Fusion you don't pick a model: it routes each prompt to one model, starts with a cheaper draft and escalates, or has other models critique a draft.
  • Choose an effort level: Fusion Standard, Fusion High or Fusion Max. Fusion is in VS Code, Copilot CLI, the GitHub Copilot app and JetBrains IDEs, not yet on github.com.
  • No extra charge for Fusion: each model call is billed at that model's normal rate in GitHub AI Credits, and your organization's model policies still apply.
